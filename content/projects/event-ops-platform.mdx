## Problem

Event operations lived across five tools: spreadsheets for schedules and vendors, email threads for
approvals, and no single source of truth. Every change had to be copied by hand, and things fell
through the gaps.

<Todo>expand the problem: who the users are and what broke in the old workflow.</Todo>

## Approach

- **One data model.** PostgreSQL and Prisma replaced the spreadsheets, with Zod validating every
  write at the boundary.
- **A careful AI agent.** The Claude-powered agent turns a plain-English request into a draft
  schedule, validates it against live data through a custom tool, and fixes its own conflicts.
  A spending cap bounds cost, and a human approves each block before it's saved.
- **Safe migrations.** All 13 schema migrations are reversible and run through a dry-run
  harness first. It caught 5 data-loss defects before they reached production.
