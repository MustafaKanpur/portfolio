## Problem

Insurance and pension contributions vary widely across Canada, but the data that could explain
why is spread across large Environics datasets that are hard to join and slow to work with.

<Todo>expand the problem: the original team brief and what you extended on your own.</Todo>

## Approach

- **Pipeline.** A Polars pipeline merges 4.6 GB of Environics data across 77,542 postal codes
  and about 970 features.
- **Model.** XGBoost regression reached R² 0.861, against a 0.41 baseline, with a 0.44-point MAE
  on 11,632 held-out postal codes. SHAP explains which features drive contributions.
- **Dashboard.** A four-page Power BI report built with Power Query, a one-to-one data model, and
  income-weighted DAX measures.
